I PUC ELECTRONICS

Build the Foundation

The I PUC curriculum introduces the fundamental concepts, components, measurement techniques, circuits, semiconductors and digital electronics.

Introduction to Electronics

Components, semiconductor devices, ICs, microprocessors, microcontrollers and applications.

Digital Electronics

Digital and binary signals, number systems, Boolean algebra and logic gates.

Measurement Instruments

Voltmeter, ammeter, ohmmeter, multimeter and oscilloscope.

Electricity & Network Principles

Charge, current, potential difference, Ohm's law, power, Kirchhoff's laws, network theorems and AC.

Passive Components

Resistors, capacitors, inductors, transformers and transducers.

Semiconductors & Diodes

PN junction, biasing, junction diode, Zener diode, rectifiers, filters, regulated supplies and LED.

Transistors & Digital Logic

BJT, configurations, basic logic gates, NAND/NOR, practical verification and PCB development.

II PUC ELECTRONICS

Explore Advanced Electronics

II PUC builds on the foundation of I PUC and introduces amplifiers, digital systems, communication, power electronics, microcontrollers and programming.

FET & Transistors

Field-effect transistors and transistor-based electronic systems.

Transistor Amplifiers

Amplifier principles and transistor amplifier circuits.

Digital Electronics

XOR/XNOR, universal gates, adders/subtractors, Boolean simplification, K-maps and sequential circuits.

Operational Amplifiers

Inverting/non-inverting amplifiers, voltage follower, summing amplifier, integrator, filters and comparator.

Oscillators

Sinusoidal oscillators, LC, RC, crystal and Wien bridge oscillators.

Communication

Wireless communication, AM/FM, demodulation, mobile networks, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, optical fibre, satellite communication and RADAR.

Power Electronics

SCR, TRIAC, MOSFET, power converters and AC-to-DC.

Microcontrollers

8051 architecture, registers, addressing modes, instruction set and assembly language.

C Programming

C programming fundamentals and basic programs.
